Abilities

1. Disengaging Sigil Radius 6.5m

Draw a sigil sphere in front of you and then leap backwards as it explodes, damaging and slowing enemies caught in it.

Against it: Leave the sigil blast area and don't overchase Apollo after he leaps back

2. Riposte 35m

Prepare to deflect the next incoming attack. On a successful deflection, briefly become invulnerable and target an enemy hero to dash towards them, stunning them and reducing their Melee Resist.Press Ability 2 to select a target.Does not trigger against trooper or neutral damage.

Against it: Don't shoot while Riposte is up; wait for the stance to drop

3. Flawless Advance 5m

Perform a series of lunges in any direction, delivering piercing stabs ahead of you. Hold your ability key to time your attacks, dealing more damage the longer it's held. Releasing your attack during the perfect window deals maximum damage.Press Ability 3 to re-cast.

Against it: Watch the lunge direction and sidestep

4. Itani Lo Sahn 27m

Charge up and perform a long range slash. Struck enemies cannot take action or heal and are stuck in slow motion. When this effect expires, they suffer devastating damage, dealing bonus damage against half-health enemies.While in slow motion, Apollo is invulnerable and enemies take reduced damage.Hold Ability 4 or to delay the cast.

Against it: When you see the slash wind up, step out of its line

Gun
Apollo's gun

Single shot · Ammo 15 · 2.6 bullets/sec · DPS 49 · full damage to 25m, minimum from 25m

Playing against

  • Stop attacking when he braces for Riposte and wait it out
  • After he leaps away with Disengaging Sigil he has fewer ways in, so push then
  • Below half health, get far away from the ultimate slash

Counter items

Slowing HexDebuff Reducer

Strengths

  • A successful Riposte makes him invulnerable and stuns
  • Flawless Advance moves and attacks at once
  • Ultimate stops enemy actions and healing

Weaknesses

  • Missing the Riposte timing puts him at risk
  • Has to get close, weak to long-range poke
  • Hard to play and easy to misplay

Game plan

Laning

Deflect attacks with Riposte and trade with short lunges.

Mid game

Flank with Flawless Advance and pick off lone enemies.

Late game

Lock down key enemies with the ultimate and finish them below half health.

Power spikes

Itani Lo Sahn lets him burst enemies below half health. His damage climbs a lot as he gets used to Flawless Advance timing.

With them on your team

  • Damage is numbed during the ultimate's slow motion, so attack right after it ends
  • Follow in right away when Riposte stuns an enemy
