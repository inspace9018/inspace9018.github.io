Abilities

1. Scorn Radius 9m

Deal damage to nearby enemies and heal yourself based on the damage done. Heal is stronger against enemy heroes.

Against it: It heals off nearby damage, so don't stay close to him for long

2. Burrow Radius 5m

Burrow underground, moving faster, and gaining spirit and bullet armor. Damage from enemy heroes will reduce the speed bonus. When you jump out, deal spirit damage, slow, and knockup.

Against it: Hitting him while he comes underground slows him, so keep shooting

3. Sand Blast 25m

Spray sand that disarms enemies in front of you and deals damage.

Against it: You can't shoot when hit, so make distance meanwhile

4. Combo 4m

Hold the target in place, stunning them and dealing damage during the channel. If they die during, or within 3 seconds of Combo ending, you permanently gain max health.

Against it: A caught ally can't escape alone, so break it by stunning Mo & Krill

Gun
Mo & Krill's gun

4 pellets · Ammo 20 · 5.3 bullets/sec · DPS 60 · full damage to 20m, minimum from 58m

Playing against

  • When Mo & Krill aren't visible, expect a Burrow ambush
  • Stun Mo & Krill to free an ally caught in Combo
  • Back off when Sand Blast disarms you

Counter items

Healbane

Strengths

  • Burrow closes in fast for ambushes
  • Combo reliably locks down one enemy
  • Scorn's healing keeps him going in melee

Weaknesses

  • Struggles against long-range poke
  • Can't move himself during Combo
  • Taking hits while burrowed slows him down

Game plan

Laning

Stay healthy with Scorn and pressure up close.

Mid game

Burrow to other lanes for ambushes and set up kills with Combo.

Late game

Burrow behind the enemy and lock their key damage dealer in Combo.

Power spikes

Scorn gives him good staying power in early melee fights. Once he has Combo, he reliably locks down one enemy for a kill, and each Combo kill raises his max health.

With them on your team

  • Add damage on the enemy held by Combo
  • Go in together with the Burrow ambush
