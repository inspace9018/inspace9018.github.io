Abilities

1. Rake 10m

Against it: Keep your distance and watch for the combo that follows Rake

2. Sanguine Retreat 9m

Briefly disperse, becoming untargetable and flying to a target location. Can be recast within a brief window.

Against it: Attacks don't land during Retreat, so aim at where she lands

3. Love Bites

Your bullets apply additional spirit damage. Dealing damage with your abilities builds up to a vicious bite, dealing a burst of bonus spirit damage. Love Bites has a unique cooldown per target.

Against it: Hit and run briefly so you don't take ability hits in a row

4. Nox Nostra 40m

Active: Unleash a cloud of bats that seek out targets, each dealing spirit damage and applying Silence.Passive: Triggering Love Bites against heroes permanently increases the number of bats released.

Against it: When bats fly in, hide behind cover to avoid the silence

Gun
Mina's gun

Single shot · Ammo 12 · 4 bullets/sec · DPS 29 · full damage to 20m, minimum from 46m

Playing against

  • Go in once you see Sanguine Retreat is used
  • Repeated ability hits trigger Love Bites, so keep trades short
  • When bats come, use your key abilities before you get silenced

Counter items

Debuff ReducerSpirit Resilience

Strengths

  • Bullets carry Spirit damage for steady output
  • Sanguine Retreat gets her out of danger fast
  • Bat silence shuts down several enemies' abilities

Weaknesses

  • Few ways to escape after Retreat
  • Her ultimate takes time to get strong
  • Struggles when enemies get close

Game plan

Laning

Poke with bonus bullet damage and mix in abilities to trigger Love Bites.

Mid game

Trigger Love Bites on heroes often to add more bats.

Late game

Open team fights with the Nox Nostra silence and escape safely with Retreat.

Power spikes

Nox Nostra grows stronger as more bats are added by triggering Love Bites on heroes. From mid game, the ultimate's silence decides team fights.

With them on your team

  • Go in together with Mina's bat silence
  • When Mina retreats, don't force it; back off with her
