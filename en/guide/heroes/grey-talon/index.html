Abilities

1. Charged Shot

Charge up a powerful shot that pierces through enemies. Hold Ability 1 or to hold the shot.

Against it: It pierces enemies, so even behind Troopers isn't safe; stay near cover

2. Rain of Arrows

Launches you high in the air, allowing you to glide slowly. While airborne, you gain Weapon Damage and multishot on your weapon. for reduced jump height. Press Jump / mantle to cancel the glide.

Against it: He's easy to kill when you focus him in the air

3. Spirit Snare Radius 6.5m

Throw out a trap that begins to arm itself. Once armed, the trap will trigger when an enemy enters its radius, applying Curse and Movement Slow that interrupts, Silences, Disarms, and prevents item usage.Hit the trap with a Charged Shot to detonate early with increased radius.

Against it: It stops your abilities and items, so avoid the area around snares

4. Guided Owl Radius 12m

After 1.5s cast time, launch a spirit owl that you control and which explodes on impact, damaging and stunning enemies. Hold [W] to accelerate the owl.Press Jump / mantle to release control. Gain permanent Spirit Power for each enemy hero killed with Guided Owl.

Against it: It flies in after the cast: hide behind cover and go for his body while he steers

Gun
Grey Talon's gun

Single shot · Ammo 17 · 1.7 bullets/sec · DPS 39 · full damage to 18m, minimum from 54m

Playing against

  • Charged Shot pierces, so move behind cover
  • Don't walk into paths with snares; go around
  • Go for his body while he steers the owl

Counter items

Debuff Reducer

Strengths

  • Charged Shot pokes hard from far away
  • Spirit Snare shuts down enemies who dive in
  • Guided Owl stuns enemies even at a distance

Weaknesses

  • Few answers when enemies get close
  • Floats in the air during Rain of Arrows and becomes a target
  • His body is defenseless while he steers the owl

Game plan

Laning

Poke with Charged Shot and farm safely.

Mid game

Poke from high ground and help kills in other lanes with the owl.

Late game

Deal long-range damage from behind allies and block entry paths with snares.

Power spikes

Charged Shot gives strong long-range poke from the start. Once he has Guided Owl, he can stun and finish enemies even far away.

With them on your team

  • Stand in front and stop enemies diving Talon while he pokes
  • Follow up right away on enemies stunned by the owl and finish them
