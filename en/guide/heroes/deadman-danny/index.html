Abilities

1. Strix Salvo 35m

Launch a salvo of spirit missiles that lodge into the ground along a line. The missiles explode after a delay or upon detecting an enemy, dealing spirit damage and marking the target.Shooting a marked enemy with a charged shot consumes a mark to deal bonus spirit damage and apply a fading slow.Hold and drag to select the angle.

Against it: Don't step on the missile line; go around it

2. Chimera Mk1 Radius 8m

Hold Jump / mantle to fly, building up heat. When max heat is reached, your jetpack will begin to overheat.Overheating: After a short delay, dive to the ground. Upon impact, create an explosion that deals damage and launches you and nearby enemies. If no hero is hit, you stun yourself. Press Ability 2 to dive early.Cooling Down: After overheating, the jetpack cannot be used until it fully cools do

Against it: Dodge his dive landing spot and Danny stuns himself

3. Jettison Fuel 22m

Spray fuel dealing weapon damage over time and shredding bullet resist, building up to a huge backblast that deals spirit damage and knocks you backwards.Vents the jetpack when dealing damage.

Against it: Fuel shreds Bullet Resist, so get distance right away

4. Close The Deal 10m

Grab an enemy and give them a hands-on demonstration, flying high into the air before crashing back to the ground.You and the participant take spirit damage over time while falling and spirit damage upon impact. Damage is increased to enemies under 50% health.

Against it: Below half health, keep your distance so Danny can't grab you

Gun
Deadman Danny's gun

Single shot · Ammo 14 · 4 bullets/sec · DPS 60 · full damage to 18m, minimum from 47m

Playing against

  • Walk around the line of missiles in the ground
  • When the jetpack overheats, stay far from where he dives
  • Danny takes damage from his ultimate too, so keep your health up and you can turn it

Counter items

Bullet ResilienceSpirit Resilience

Strengths

  • Jetpack lets him fight from high ground
  • Missile marks boost his charged shot damage
  • Ultimate grabs an enemy for big damage

Weaknesses

  • Stuns himself if his dive misses a hero
  • Easy target when he hovers too long
  • Takes damage himself when he uses his ultimate

Game plan

Laning

Lay missile marks on paths and poke by popping them with charged shots.

Mid game

Take high ground with the jetpack and hit enemies from the flank.

Late game

After team fights, grab low-health enemies with Close The Deal and finish them.

Power spikes

Close The Deal lets him grab and finish low-health enemies. Charged shots that pop marks hit hard once weapon items stack up.

With them on your team

  • Herd marked enemies toward Danny so he can shoot them
  • Attack enemies launched by his dive together
