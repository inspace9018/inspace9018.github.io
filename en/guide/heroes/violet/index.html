Abilities

1. Living Ink 25m

Against it: Keep head-on fights with Violet short

2. Flow State Radius 15m

Drag a brush that continues to travel forward, leaving a trail that grants bonus slide distance for allies and a fading slow and spirit damage to enemies it passes through, increased the further it travels. Re-activate the ability to flick the brush forward, ending the ability early but dealing spirit damage in a larger area.

Against it: Step out of the brush trail to avoid the slow

3. Still Life 25m

Choose a hero to be your subject, dealing spirit damage over time as long as you are looking at them. If the sketch is completed they take additional spirit damage, suffer from stun, and drop a sketch that can be picked up. For each collected sketch, you permanently gain stats based on the hero.

Against it: Hide behind a wall to break line of sight and stop the sketch

4. Abstract Landscape Radius 18m

Create a circular display of ink that blocks vision and grants allies Spirit.Enemies inside are revealed, and leaving causes them to take spirit damage and suffer from Silence and slow, destroying the ink in the process.

Against it: Use the abilities you need before leaving the ink

Gun
Violet's gun

Single shot · Ammo 15 · 1.5 bullets/sec · DPS 46 · full damage to 20m, minimum from 64m

Playing against

  • When Still Life is on you, hide behind cover to break her line of sight
  • Pressure Violet so she can't pick up dropped sketches
  • Leaving the ink silences you, so use key abilities before you leave

Counter items

Debuff ReducerSpirit Resilience

Strengths

  • Grows permanently stronger as she collects sketches
  • Brush trail slows enemies and helps allies
  • Ultimate traps enemies and silences them when they leave

Weaknesses

  • Still Life needs her to keep looking at the target
  • Slow growth if she can't collect sketches
  • Little mobility, weak to ambushes

Game plan

Laning

Target enemy heroes with Still Life to collect sketches and poke with the brush.

Mid game

Grab sketches in every fight to keep raising your stats.

Late game

Trap enemies with Abstract Landscape and win fights with the ally Spirit buff.

Power spikes

Her stats rise permanently the more sketches she collects with Still Life. Abstract Landscape lets her cover a fight area in ink and silence enemies.

With them on your team

  • Slide on the brush trail to travel farther
  • Enemies inside the ink are revealed, so focus them together
