Abilities

1. Fire Scarabs

Infest an enemy with fire scarabs, stealing life from them and causing them to deal reduced damage.

Against it: Your damage is reduced while infested, so back off until it ends

2. Dust Devil 20m

Transform yourself into a whirlwind that travels forward, damaging enemies, slowing their move speed and lifting them up in the air. After emerging from the tornado you gain bullet evasion.

Against it: Step aside when the whirlwind comes, then fight back with abilities rather than guns

3. Djinn's Mark 40m

Active: Consume all Djinn Marks to deal their damage now.

Against it: Don't keep taking hits with marks stacked; get behind cover

4. Traveler Radius 30m

Target a location on the minimap. After a brief wait, teleport to that location. Taking damage during the wait period causes the teleport to be interrupted.

Against it: Hit Mirage while he waits to teleport to cancel it

Gun
Mirage's gun

Single shot · Ammo 16 · 2.7 bullets/sec · DPS 40 · full damage to 20m, minimum from 58m

Playing against

  • He gains bullet evasion after Dust Devil, so answer with abilities
  • Avoid head-on fights while infested by scarabs
  • When Mirage vanishes from lane, warn your team he may Traveler in

Counter items

HealbaneBullet Resilience

Strengths

  • Fire Scarabs cut enemy damage and steal health
  • Dust Devil engages and lifts enemies for easy combos
  • Traveler lets him join any fight fast

Weaknesses

  • Few ways to escape after Dust Devil
  • Traveler is interrupted by damage while waiting
  • Reliable crowd control is short

Game plan

Laning

Stay healthy with Fire Scarabs and poke, stacking marks with your gun.

Mid game

Traveler into fights in other lanes, open with Dust Devil and finish with marks.

Late game

Lift the enemy backline with Dust Devil and finish low enemies with mark bursts.

Power spikes

Mark burst finishers turn deadly from mid game, once Djinn's Mark is upgraded and gun items stack up. Traveler lets him join fights in other lanes fast.

With them on your team

  • Land abilities right away on enemies lifted by Dust Devil
  • Focus enemies infested by scarabs together
