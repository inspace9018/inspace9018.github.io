Abilities

1. Gloom Bombs Radius 3m

Throw a cluster of bombs that detonate after a delay, dealing spirit damage.Enemies hit by multiple bombs take 65% damage.

Against it: They explode after a delay, so leave where the bombs land at once

2. Leaping Slash 9m

Dash forward before slashing all enemies in a circle, dealing melee damage. If the ability hits at least one hero, heal a small amount of health.

Against it: She dashes in and slashes around her, so keep distance and step aside

3. Ava

Turn to shadows and possess Ava. You gain bonus move speed that increases over time, and become hidden on the minimap. Taking damage from an enemy hero resets your bonus move speed and puts Ava on a brief cooldown.

Against it: Damage resets her move speed, so hit her as soon as you see her

4. Return to Shadows Radius 7.5m

Instantly turn to shadows, becoming untargetable, gaining bonus move speed, and dealing spirit damage. After a delay, return from the shadows, dealing spirit damage again.

Against it: She deals damage again when she comes out of the shadows, so move away from that spot

Gun
Calico's gun

9 pellets · Ammo 12 · 4.8 bullets/sec · DPS 79 · full damage to 20m, minimum from 58m

Playing against

  • When Calico vanishes from the minimap, stay near allies and expect an ambush
  • If she comes at you as Ava, hit her at once to break her speed
  • Don't give her melee range; back off while poking

Strengths

  • Ava hides her from the minimap for ambushes
  • Leaping Slash closes in fast and heals a little
  • Return to Shadows makes her untargetable in a pinch

Weaknesses

  • Melee-focused, weak against long-range poke
  • Taking hits as Ava resets her move speed
  • Few ways out after going in

Game plan

Laning

Poke with Gloom Bombs and trade and heal with Leaping Slash.

Mid game

Roam the map as Ava and ambush enemies who are alone.

Late game

Ambush from the flank to kill enemy damage dealers and get out with Return to Shadows.

Power spikes

Gloom Bombs let her poke in lane, and once she has Return to Shadows she survives after going in, making her ambushes much stronger.

With them on your team

  • Go in with Calico when she ambushes and add damage
  • Calico is melee, so take the enemy's long-range poke for her
