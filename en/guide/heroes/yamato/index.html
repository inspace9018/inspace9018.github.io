Abilities

1. Power Slash 22m

Channel to increase damage over 1.4 seconds, then release a fully-charged sword strike.Press Ability 1 or to trigger the strike early, dealing partial damage.

Against it: When you see the charge, get out of its path or interrupt it early

2. Flying Slash 26m

Throw a grappling hook to reel yourself towards an enemy, dealingmelee damage and slowing the target when you arrive.

Against it: She hooks in and slows you, so block the angle with cover

3. Crimson Slash Radius 13m

Slash enemies in front of you, damaging them and slowing their fire rate. If any enemy heroes are hit, you heal.

Against it: It slows your fire rate and heals Yamato, so keep your distance

4. Shadow Transformation

Become infused with Yamato's shadow soul. After an initial invincible transformation your abilities are refreshed and are 60% faster. You gain immunity to negative status effects and have greatly increased bullet and spirit resist.When you get a hero kill, you heal and the duration is extended.

Against it: Crowd control doesn't work during it, so stall and back off

Gun
Yamato's gun

5 pellets · Ammo 12 · 2.4 bullets/sec · DPS 63 · full damage to 20m, minimum from 46m

Playing against

  • Block Flying Slash hook angles with cover
  • Don't fight during Shadow Transformation; back off until it ends
  • When Power Slash starts charging, step out of its path

Counter items

Healbane

Strengths

  • Flying Slash closes in on enemies fast
  • Crimson Slash heals her while fighting
  • Immune to debuffs during Shadow Transformation

Weaknesses

  • Has to get close, weak against long-range poke
  • Easy to target while charging Power Slash
  • Much weaker once Shadow Transformation ends

Game plan

Laning

Trade with Crimson Slash and hold off on risky dives.

Mid game

Use Flying Slash on enemies who are alone to rack up kills.

Late game

Go into the enemy with Shadow Transformation and extend it with kills.

Power spikes

Crimson Slash and Flying Slash let her fight up close. Once she has Shadow Transformation, debuff immunity lets her break into team fights.

With them on your team

  • Help Yamato with slows or stuns on the enemy she's on
  • Go in with her during Shadow Transformation to chain kills
