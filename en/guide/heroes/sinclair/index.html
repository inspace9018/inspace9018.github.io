Abilities

1. Vexing Bolt very far

Fire a bolt of magic that deals Damage, increasing as it travels. If you have an Assistant, they also cast Vexing Bolt at reduced damage. Press Ability 1 to redirect the bolt towards your crosshairs.

Against it: Bolts get stronger at range, so take cover or get close

2. Spectral Assistant 15m

Summon an Assistant at the targeted location. The Assistant attacks whenever you fire your weapon, dealing Damage. While the Assistant is out, you can press Ability 2 to swap positions with your Assistant. Casting Spectral Assistant also reloads your weapon.

Against it: Shoot the assistant first when you see it

3. Rabbit Hex 24m

Hex a target area and transform all enemies into a Rabbit for a limited duration. Rabbits are small and move faster, but take increased Damage and are unable to perform most actions.Hex does not interrupt abilities.

Against it: As a rabbit, use the extra speed to run to your allies

4. Audience Participation 20m

Copy the Ultimate of an enemy hero for a limited time. Reactivating the ability will use the Copied Ultimate instead.Copies will inherit this ability's upgrade points.Audience Participation will also copy the cooldown.

Against it: Your own ultimate can be copied, so watch for it after he casts

Gun
Sinclair's gun

2-round burst · Ammo 16 · 2.7 bullets/sec · DPS 45 · full damage to 20m, minimum from 60m

Playing against

  • Bolts hurt more the farther they fly, so take cover or close the gap
  • Kill the assistant first to stop its extra damage and swaps
  • When turned into a rabbit, use the extra speed to fall back to allies

Counter items

Spirit ResilienceDebuff Reducer

Strengths

  • Vexing Bolt gives strong long-range poke
  • Rabbit Hex disables several enemies at once
  • Swaps places with the assistant to escape danger

Weaknesses

  • Relies on abilities, weak while they're on cooldown
  • Hard to play and easy to misplay
  • Fragile and vulnerable to ambushes

Game plan

Laning

Chip enemies steadily with Vexing Bolt and add gun damage with the assistant.

Mid game

Lock enemies with Rabbit Hex and set up kills with your team.

Late game

Copy the most dangerous enemy ultimate and turn the team fight.

Power spikes

Team fight control jumps once Rabbit Hex is upgraded. Audience Participation is worth more the more strong ultimates the enemy has.

With them on your team

  • Rabbits take more damage, so focus them right away
  • Time your engage with the ultimate Sinclair copied
