Abilities

1. Rend 16m

Swipe at enemies in a cone ahead of you, dealing spirit damage. If the enemy is in close range, deal bonus melee damage.

Against it: Rend hurts more up close, so don't let him get near

2. Stalker's Mark

Send out a mark that bleeds the first target it hits, dealing spirit damage over time.While the enemy is bleeding you can re-activate Stalker's Mark to instantly appear behind their back.

Against it: If the mark makes you bleed, expect Drifter to appear behind you

3. Bloodscent 80m

Against it: When low, don't stay alone near Drifter

4. Eternal Night very far

Surround nearby enemy heroes in darkness, severely limiting their vision of other units. Affected enemies are briefly revealed to you and are considered isolated for the duration.You gain bonus sprint speed.

Against it: Trapped in darkness, use the minimap and sound to move to allies

Gun
Drifter's gun

3 pellets · Ammo 12 · 2.3 bullets/sec · DPS 133 · full damage to 22m, minimum from 27m

Playing against

  • Dodge the mark projectile to stop the teleport ambush
  • While bleeding, stay close to allies in case he appears behind you
  • In Eternal Night, don't move alone; group up with allies

Counter items

Spirit ResilienceDebuff Reducer

Strengths

  • Teleports behind a marked enemy to ambush
  • Rend deals big bonus damage up close
  • Ultimate heavily blocks enemy vision

Weaknesses

  • Fewer ways to engage if the mark misses
  • Weak against enemies poking from range
  • Must get close, so vulnerable to crowd control

Game plan

Laning

Trade briefly with Rend and the mark, and get behind bleeding enemies to pressure them.

Mid game

Mark lone enemies and catch them fast.

Late game

Isolate enemies with Eternal Night and take out key damage dealers first.

Power spikes

Once Stalker's Mark and Rend combos are in place, he catches lone enemies fast. Eternal Night lets him split enemies apart in team fights.

With them on your team

  • When Drifter gets behind enemies, pressure them from the front
  • Focus enemies blinded by Eternal Night
