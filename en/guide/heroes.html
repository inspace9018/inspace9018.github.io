Abilities

1. Napalm 20m

Spew an incendiary mixture, dealing spirit damage, applying slow, and coating targets in napalm.

Against it: Sprays forward: step aside, and back off if it hits you

2. Flame Dash Radius 4.5m

Dash forward, gaining slow resistance while leaving a flaming trail that deals spirit damage over time. Hold [W] while active to dash farther.

Against it: Don't stand in the fire left behind the dash; move aside

3. Afterburn

Weapon hits build up a burning effect, dealing spirit damage over time. Abilities refresh to the base burn duration and weapon hits extend it.

Against it: Burn lasts longer the more bullets you take, so don't trade for long

4. Concussive Combustion Radius 12m

Become a living bomb, dealing spirit damage and applying stun to all nearby enemies after a delay.Once cast, Concussive Combustion cannot be interrupted.

Against it: Can't be interrupted once cast: leave the radius as soon as you see it

Gun
Infernus's gun

Single shot · Ammo 27 · 9.5 bullets/sec · DPS 52 · full damage to 18m, minimum from 55m

Playing against

  • Back off right after Napalm hits so the burn can't be refreshed
  • When Concussive Combustion starts, dash out of the radius at once
  • Fight back right after Flame Dash, when he has no way out

Counter items

Spirit ShieldingDebuff Reducer

Strengths

  • Every bullet stacks burn, so short trades go his way
  • Flame Dash makes engaging and escaping easy
  • Ultimate stuns several enemies at once

Weaknesses

  • Short range, weak at long-range poke
  • Hard to escape once Flame Dash is used
  • Ultimate has a delay before it goes off and can be dodged

Game plan

Laning

Chip enemy health with Afterburn and clear waves with Napalm.

Mid game

Use Flame Dash to rotate to other lanes, ganking and farming the jungle.

Late game

Dive in from the flank with Flame Dash and open fights with Concussive Combustion.

Power spikes

Strong poke from the start thanks to Afterburn and Napalm. Concussive Combustion gives a team fight engage, and fight damage climbs fast as duration and ammo items come in.

With them on your team

  • Lock enemies down and Infernus stacks burn easily
  • Go in as the ultimate goes off and finish the stunned enemies
