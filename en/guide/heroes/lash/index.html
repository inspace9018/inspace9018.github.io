Abilities

1. Ground Strike Radius 10m

Stomp the ground beneath you, damaging enemies in front of you. If you perform Ground Strike while airborne, you quickly dive towards the ground. Damage grows slower after 25m.

Against it: It's stronger the farther he falls, so don't stand under Lash in the air

2. Grapple 30m

Pull yourself through the air toward a target. Using Grapple also resets your limit of air jumps and dashes.

Against it: Counter right after he flies in on the whip

3. Flog 20m

Strike enemies in front of you with your whip, healing for a portion of the damage dealt.

Against it: Lash heals when it hits, so don't give him melee range

4. Death Slam 20m

Focus on enemies to connect whips to them. After channeling, connected enemies are lifted and stunned then slammed into the ground. Your victims and any enemies in the landing zone will be damaged and slowed. Press to throw connected enemies early. Enemies that are not in line of sight or go out of range during the latch time will not be grabbed.

Against it: Leave his line of sight or range during the channel and you won't be caught

Gun
Lash's gun

3-round burst · Ammo 29 · 5.8 bullets/sec · DPS 49 · full damage to 16m, minimum from 48m

Playing against

  • When Lash is up high, watch for the slam
  • When Death Slam starts, hide behind a wall to break line of sight
  • Fight back right after he comes in with Grapple

Counter items

Spirit Shielding

Strengths

  • Grapple gives him very high mobility
  • Ground Strike slams down from high ground
  • Death Slam stuns several enemies

Weaknesses

  • Few ways out after going in
  • Death Slam can be interrupted while channeling
  • Breaking line of sight is enough to dodge Death Slam

Game plan

Laning

Pressure by hitting the wave and the enemy together with Ground Strike.

Mid game

Roam the map with Grapple and gank.

Late game

Come in from high ground and throw several enemies with Death Slam.

Power spikes

Ground Strike and Flog let him fight early. Once he has Death Slam, he gets a team fight move that lifts and throws several enemies.

With them on your team

  • Use AoE abilities on enemies Lash throws with Death Slam
  • Go in with Lash when he slams down
