Abilities

1. Kinetic Pulse 16m

Release an energy pulse that travels along the ground, dealing spirit damage and applying knockup.

Against it: The pulse travels along the ground, so step aside from its path

2. Quantum Entanglement 10m

Briefly become untargetable while teleporting to the target location. Restores stamina upon use. : Bring nearby allies with you.

Against it: An engage follows right after the teleport, so watch for his ultimate

3. Rejuvenating Aurora Radius 8m

While channeling, restore health over time to you and any allies nearby.You can dash and melee without breaking the channel.

Against it: Use healing reduction on enemies who are being healed

4. Singularity Radius 7m

Create a singularity in your hands, dealing spirit damage over time, applying stun, and pulling in nearby enemies.

Against it: It pulls in nearby enemies, so don't group up near Dynamo

Gun
Dynamo's gun

Single shot · Ammo 20 · 3.8 bullets/sec · DPS 48 · full damage to 20m, minimum from 58m

Playing against

  • When Dynamo comes close, spread out instead of grouping
  • Beat the aurora's healing with healing reduction or burst
  • Poke him hard in lane to slow his growth

Counter items

Healbane

Strengths

  • Singularity pulls in and stuns several enemies
  • Quantum Entanglement teleports him along with allies
  • Rejuvenating Aurora heals nearby allies

Weaknesses

  • Low damage, so little lane pressure
  • Loses much of his impact if Singularity is interrupted
  • Weak offense while channeling the aurora

Game plan

Laning

Poke with Kinetic Pulse and help your lane partner farm in comfort.

Mid game

Join ganks in the next lane with Quantum Entanglement.

Late game

Engage from the flank or above with Quantum Entanglement and open fights with Singularity.

Power spikes

His lane pressure is weak, but his team fight impact rises sharply once he has Singularity. With cooldown and Vitality items he can go for his ultimate more often.

With them on your team

  • Use your AoE abilities on enemies gathered by Singularity
  • Step inside Rejuvenating Aurora's range to get healed
