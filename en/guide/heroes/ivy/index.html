Abilities

1. Entangling Thorns Radius 6m

Summon a patch of choking thorns that damages and slows enemies in its radius.

Against it: You're slowed inside the thorns, so step out at once

2. Kudzu Connection 16m

Connect with a nearby ally to gain bonuses, replicated healing, and ignore the move speed penalty while shooting.Receive 50% of Bonuses with no connectionConnection requires line of sight.

Against it: The link needs line of sight, so break it with cover between Ivy and her ally

3. Stone Form Radius 5.8m

Turn yourself into impervious stone and smash into the ground, stunning and damaging enemies nearby. Heals you for a percentage of your max health.

Against it: Save your attacks during Stone Form and back out of the stun radius

4. Air Drop 22m

Grab an ally and take flight with them. Drop your ally to cause an explosion, dealing spirit damage. You and your ally gain increased Outgoing Damage when flying ends. Cooldown reduced by 30% when used on allies.Taking damage briefly disables using the ability.While lifted, your ally cannot attack and deals -20% damage.

Against it: Damage briefly blocks it, so keep hitting Ivy

Gun
Ivy's gun

Single shot · Ammo 33 · 13.6 bullets/sec · DPS 61 · full damage to 20m, minimum from 58m

Playing against

  • Don't stand in the thorn patch; get out at once
  • Break line of sight between Ivy and her ally with cover to stop the link
  • Stone Form stuns, so don't stand right next to Ivy

Strengths

  • Kudzu Connection shares bonuses and healing with an ally
  • Stone Form stuns nearby enemies and heals her
  • Air Drop lets her ambush together with an ally

Weaknesses

  • Bonuses are halved with no ally to link to
  • The link breaks when line of sight is cut
  • Taking damage briefly blocks Air Drop

Game plan

Laning

Poke with the thorn patch and keep linked to your lane partner.

Mid game

Carry an ally with Air Drop and look for ambushes in other lanes.

Late game

Link to your key ally to boost them and stop divers with Stone Form.

Power spikes

Kudzu Connection makes her strong when fighting next to an ally. Once she has Air Drop, she can fly an ally in for an ambush.

With them on your team

  • Fight where you keep line of sight with Ivy to hold the link
  • You can't attack while carried by Air Drop, so deal damage right after landing
