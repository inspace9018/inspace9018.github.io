Abilities

1. Pulse Grenade Radius 5.5m

Throw a grenade that begins pulsing when it lands. Each pulse expands the radius and applies spirit damage, time slow, and stacking increased damage for Paradox against the victim.

Against it: Each pulse widens the radius, so leave as soon as it lands

2. Time Wall very far

Create a time warping wall that stops time for all enemy projectiles and bullets that touch it and increases the speed and damage of friendly bullets. Enemies that touch the wall will be briefly slowed.

Against it: Bullets that touch the wall stop, so go around it to attack

3. Kinetic Carbine

Charge up a powerful shot of time energy, dealing spirit damage and applying a Time-Stop to enemies hit. The damage dealt increases with weapon damage. Move speed is increased while charging. While in the air and charging, to timeslow your movement.

Against it: She moves faster while charging, so take cover when you see the charge

4. Paradoxical Swap 25m

Fire a projectile that swaps your position with the target enemy hero.

Against it: Dodge the projectile and you won't be swapped, so keep strafing

Gun
Paradox's gun

5-round burst · Ammo 40 · 7.6 bullets/sec · DPS 51 · full damage to 20m, minimum from 58m

Playing against

  • When you see Kinetic Carbine charging, hide behind cover
  • Strafe left and right to dodge the Paradoxical Swap projectile
  • Go after Paradox right after the swap, while she's stuck on your side

Counter items

Debuff Reducer

Strengths

  • Kinetic Carbine stops time from long range
  • Paradoxical Swap pulls enemies toward her team
  • Time Wall stops enemy projectiles and bullets

Weaknesses

  • Hard to play, relies heavily on landing abilities
  • Left on the enemy side after Paradoxical Swap
  • Weak in trades when abilities are down

Game plan

Laning

Poke with Pulse Grenade and the carbine, and farm safely.

Mid game

Combo Paradoxical Swap with the grenade to get kills in the next lane.

Late game

Pull the enemy carry toward your team and boost your team's damage with Time Wall.

Power spikes

Pulse Grenade and Kinetic Carbine give her early poke. Once she has Paradoxical Swap, she can pull one enemy toward her team and set up a sure kill.

With them on your team

  • Focus enemies Paradox pulls in right away
  • Shooting through Time Wall makes bullets stronger, so fight behind it
