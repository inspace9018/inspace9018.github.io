Abilities

1. Exploding Uppercut 6m

Deal melee damage to nearby enemies and apply knockback. When they land, they deal spirit damage and apply reduced fire rate to other nearby enemies. Exploding Uppercut can be used on allies.

Against it: Short-range hit, so don't get too close to Bebop

2. Sticky Bomb 6m

Attach a bomb that explodes after a delay, dealing spirit damage to nearby enemies. If the bomb hits or kills a hero, you gain permanent bonus damage on Sticky Bomb. Stacks diminish by half after 60 hits and 7 kills

Against it: With a bomb on you, move away from allies before it explodes

3. Grapple Arm 30m

Launch out a mechanical hand that pulls the first character it hits, reeling them in. Grapple Arm can be used on allies.

Against it: Only the first target hit gets pulled, so stand behind Troopers

4. Hyper Beam 70m

Channel a powerful torrent of energy that deals spirit damage and applies slow.

Against it: Step sideways out of the beam and use cover

Gun
Bebop's gun

Single shot · Ammo 66 · 11.9 bullets/sec · DPS 59 · full damage to 20m, minimum from 51m

Playing against

  • Stand behind Troopers to block Grapple Arm
  • With a Sticky Bomb on you, move away from allies before it explodes
  • Step sideways or behind cover to avoid Hyper Beam

Counter items

Spirit Shielding

Strengths

  • Grapple Arm pulls enemies in for a sure combo
  • Sticky Bomb grows permanently the more it hits
  • Hyper Beam deals sustained damage over a wide area

Weaknesses

  • Few ways to engage if the hook misses
  • Damage stops if Hyper Beam's channel is interrupted
  • Grows slowly if Sticky Bomb doesn't land

Game plan

Laning

Look for hook angles between Troopers and pressure with the uppercut combo.

Mid game

Keep landing Sticky Bomb to grow it while you gank other lanes.

Late game

Hook in the enemy's key hero and deal team fight damage with Hyper Beam.

Power spikes

Grapple Arm into Exploding Uppercut is a threat from the start. Sticky Bomb grows with every hit, so his burst gets bigger into mid and late game.

With them on your team

  • Unload your abilities on the enemy Bebop pulls in
  • Bebop's hook can save you, so stay in his sight when in danger
