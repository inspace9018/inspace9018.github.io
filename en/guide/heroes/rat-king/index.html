Abilities

1. Scrap Grenade Radius 8m

Throw a grenade of scrap that detonates multiple times, dealing bullet damage and applying slow.The grenade can be meleed to knock it away.

Against it: Melee the grenade away when it lands

2. Rat Swarm 50m

Call forth a swarm of rats that leap onto enemies, dealing spirit damage over time and reducing their bullet resist with every bite. The more rats are attached, the faster they will bite the target.Enemies can have multiple rats attached, but can shake off a rat early by performing a ground or air dash.

Against it: Dash on the ground or in the air to shake off rats

3. Royal Pestments

Adorn scrappy armor, gaining barrier based on your max health. As long as the armor holds, bullets have a chance to be deflected back towards the attacker.The barrier does not benefit from resists.

Against it: Bullets bounce back while the armor holds, so use abilities

4. Rule, Ratannia! Radius 20m

Charge up and summon the banner of Ratannia, granting all nearby allies increased move speed, immunity from slows, and reduced incoming damage while near the banner.

Against it: Don't fight near the banner; pull enemies away from it

Gun
Rat King's gun

2 pellets · Ammo 24 · 3.3 bullets/sec · DPS 40 · full damage to 20m, minimum from 50m

Playing against

  • Dash right away to shake off rats
  • Shoot less while his armor is up and break it with abilities
  • Melee the grenade away or leave its radius

Counter items

Spirit ResilienceDebuff Reducer

Strengths

  • Armor barrier deflects bullets back at attackers
  • Rat Swarm shreds enemy Bullet Resist
  • Banner gives allies slow immunity and damage reduction

Weaknesses

  • Rats can be shaken off with a dash
  • Scrap Grenade can be meleed away
  • Buffs fade away from the banner

Game plan

Laning

Poke with Scrap Grenade and Rat Swarm and soak damage with the armor.

Mid game

Take down enemies with shredded resist alongside your damage dealers.

Late game

Plant the banner, go in with your team and take damage up front.

Power spikes

His ultimate banner makes the whole team tougher and faster in fights. His scrap armor barrier grows as Vitality items stack up.

With them on your team

  • Fight near the banner for move speed and damage reduction
  • Enemies with rats on them have low Bullet Resist, so focus fire
