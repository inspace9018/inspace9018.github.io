Abilities

1. Bookwyrm 20m

Conjure a dragon that appears at the target location before flying forward, dealing spirit damage and leaving a burning path in its trail.

Against it: Step aside from the dragon's path and the fire it leaves

2. Plot Armor 35m

Grant an ally a barrier. While the barrier holds, they gain bonus weapon damage.

Against it: Don't fight barriered enemies head-on; wait for the barrier to fade

3. Captivating Read 30m

Target an area with latent magic, applying slow. The magic detonates after a delay, dealing spirit damage and applying immobilize.

Against it: Immobilize follows the slow, so leave the area at once

4. Rallying Charge very far

Release a wave of spectral knights and steeds that charge across the entire city, healing allies and dealing spirit damage to enemies. The strength of the heal and spirit damage is amplified as the knights travel further.

Against it: Knights hit harder the farther they travel, so step out of the path when low

Gun
Paige's gun

Single shot · Ammo 14 · 1.7 bullets/sec · DPS 58 · full damage to 20m, minimum from 58m

Playing against

  • When hit by Captivating Read, leave the area before it detonates
  • Barriered enemies gain weapon damage, so break the barrier first
  • Don't stand in the fire the dragon leaves behind

Counter items

HealbaneSpirit Resilience

Strengths

  • Plot Armor protects and empowers allies
  • Captivating Read locks enemies for easy combos
  • Ultimate heals and attacks across the whole map

Weaknesses

  • Weak at getting kills alone
  • No movement ability, vulnerable to ambushes
  • Delayed blasts are easy to dodge

Game plan

Laning

Give allies Plot Armor to win trades and poke with Bookwyrm.

Mid game

Lock enemies with Captivating Read to help allies get kills.

Late game

Heal distant allies with Rallying Charge and chip enemy health before team fights.

Power spikes

Rallying Charge heals and damages across the whole map, so she affects every lane. An upgraded Plot Armor greatly helps allied damage dealers in fights.

With them on your team

  • Plot Armor boosts your weapon damage, so fight aggressively while you have it
  • Go in when Captivating Read locks enemies down
