Abilities

1. Lightning Ball Radius 4.3m

Shoot a ball of lightning that travels in a straight line. Does damage to all targets in its radius. Slows down when damaging enemies and stops if it hits the world.

Against it: Flies in a straight line: strafe aside, and walls block it

2. Static Charge 15m

Apply a charge to a target enemy hero. After a short duration, the static charge stuns and damages enemies within the radius.

Against it: If it lands on you, move away from allies before the stun so only you get hit

3. Power Surge Radius 10m

Power up your weapon with a shock effect, making your bullets proc shock damage on your target. This shock damage bounces to enemies near your target. Occurs once per burst shot.

Against it: Damage bounces to nearby enemies, so don't stand close to allies

4. Storm Cloud Radius 30m

Channel an expanding storm cloud around you that damages all enemies within its radius. Enemies do not take damage when they are out of line-of-sight.

Against it: No damage out of sight: hide behind cover or interrupt it with a stun

Gun
Seven's gun

3-round burst · Ammo 29 · 5.8 bullets/sec · DPS 63 · full damage to 20m, minimum from 58m

Playing against

  • When Static Charge lands on you, move away from allies so they don't get stunned too
  • When Storm Cloud spreads out, hide behind a wall to break line of sight
  • He has no mobility, so stay close and pressure him

Counter items

Silence WaveSpirit Shielding

Strengths

  • Lightning Ball clears waves and pokes at the same time
  • Static Charge lands a reliable stun
  • Storm Cloud deals huge AoE damage in team fights

Weaknesses

  • Loses his impact if the ultimate channel is interrupted
  • No mobility, so he struggles to escape when dived
  • Breaking line of sight avoids Storm Cloud damage

Game plan

Laning

Push the wave with Lightning Ball and hit the enemy hero with it too.

Mid game

Clear your lane fast and join the next lane to hunt kills.

Late game

Cast Storm Cloud from a safe spot and take over team fights.

Power spikes

Lightning Ball and Power Surge make his early laning very strong. Once he has Storm Cloud and Spirit items, one team fight can turn the game.

With them on your team

  • Follow up right away on enemies hit by Static Charge
  • Block the enemy engage in front of Seven while he channels his ultimate
