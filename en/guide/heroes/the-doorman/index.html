Abilities

1. Call Bell Radius 5.5m

Throw out a call bell that deals spirit damage on impact. After a short delay it explodes, dealing additional spirit damage and causing affected enemies to suffer reduced weapon accuracy and movement slow.Can be shot to detonate early.

Against it: Leave where the bell lands at once to avoid the slow and accuracy loss

2. Doorway 50m

Place two connected doors in the world. Players and most projectiles entering one door will exit out through the other. The doors will close at the end of the duration.The two doors must be placed within the doorway distance of each other.

Against it: Projectiles can come out the other door, so watch both doors

3. Luggage Cart 20m

Send out a Luggage Cart that deals spirit damage and pulls enemy heroes along its path. Can be alt cast with to target friendly heroes instead.

Against it: Step aside out of the cart's path

4. Hotel Guest 7m

Send the target's physical body to be a guest at the Baroness Hotel. The guest is to promptly make their way to the exit elevator, where they'll be sent back to their original position. A stay at the Baroness Hotel is paid for in spirit damage. Failure to check-out on time costs additional spirit damage.

Against it: In the hotel, don't hesitate; head straight for the exit

Gun
The Doorman's gun

Single shot · Ammo 8 · 1.5 bullets/sec · DPS 35 · full damage to 20m, minimum from 58m

Playing against

  • Leave the area before Call Bell explodes
  • You can use the Doorman's doors too, so turn them against him
  • When sent to the hotel, run straight for the exit elevator

Counter items

Debuff ReducerSpirit Resilience

Strengths

  • Call Bell lowers enemy accuracy and movement
  • Doorway creates routes for allies
  • Ultimate removes one enemy from the fight

Weaknesses

  • Direct damage is relatively weak
  • Enemies can use his doors once they find them
  • Weak in brawls when abilities are down

Game plan

Laning

Lower enemy accuracy with Call Bell and pull them in with the cart to attack with allies.

Mid game

Use Doorway to open ambush routes and help fights in other lanes.

Late game

Send the most dangerous enemy to the hotel and win the team fight meanwhile.

Power spikes

Hotel Guest can pull a key enemy out of the fight for a while, making team fights easier. Doorway is a big help for both allied ambushes and retreats.

With them on your team

  • When the Doorman places doors, use them to ambush or escape
  • Focus enemies pulled in by the cart right away
