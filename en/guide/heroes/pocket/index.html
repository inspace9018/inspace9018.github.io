Abilities

1. Barrage Radius 4.5m

Channel to start launching projectiles that deal spirit damage and apply slow around their impact point. Projectiles that hit a hero grant Pocket increased damage that stacks.Casting while airborne will cause Pocket to float.Casting AirDash or Flying Cloak will not cancel Barrage.

Against it: Move out of the impact area and shoot Pocket himself to pressure him

2. Flying Cloak Radius 5m

Launch a sentient cloak that travels forward and damages enemies. You can press Ability 2 to teleport to its location.

Against it: Pocket appears where the cloak ends up, so aim at that spot

3. Enchanter's Satchel Radius 12m

Escape into your suitcase. When the duration ends, deal spirit damage to nearby enemies. Duration can be ended early by performing any action.

Against it: Don't stand near the satchel; hit him the moment he comes out

4. Affliction Radius 9m

Applies damage over time to enemies nearby.Affliction's damage is non-lethal and does not apply item procs.

Against it: Affliction alone can't kill you, but it drains health, so get away at once

Gun
Pocket's gun

7 pellets · Ammo 11 · 1.9 bullets/sec · DPS 57 · full damage to 16m, minimum from 46m

Playing against

  • Keep your distance and poke from outside shotgun range
  • When he hides in the satchel, back off and hit him as he comes out
  • When the cloak flies in, predict the teleport spot and aim there

Counter items

Spirit ResilienceSilence Wave

Strengths

  • Shotgun and cloak give strong close-range burst
  • Satchel escape can dodge key abilities
  • Barrage slows grouped enemies over a wide area

Weaknesses

  • Low health, goes down fast if caught first
  • Shotgun is weak at range
  • Movement is predictable while channeling Barrage

Game plan

Laning

Poke briefly with shotgun and cloak, and pressure Troopers and enemies together with Barrage.

Mid game

Flank with the cloak teleport and pour Barrage onto grouped enemies.

Late game

Chip the whole enemy team with Affliction, then finish while dodging counters in the satchel.

Power spikes

Team fight AoE damage climbs a lot from mid game, once Spirit Power backs up Barrage. Affliction lets him chip several enemies at once.

With them on your team

  • When Pocket goes in with the cloak, follow right away and add damage
  • Focus enemies slowed by Barrage first
