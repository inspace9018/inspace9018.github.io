Abilities

1. Alchemical Flask Radius 5.5m

Throw a flask that damages and reduces the weapon damage and move speed of enemies it hits.

Against it: It cuts your weapon damage and move speed, so avoid trades meanwhile

2. Willpower

Gain a Barrier and bonus move speed.

Against it: He gains a Barrier, so avoid forcing trades right after Willpower

3. Binding Word 15m

Curse an enemy hero. If they don't move away from their initial position within the escape time, they will be damaged and immobilized.

Against it: When cursed, move away from your starting spot at once

4. Last Stand Radius 12m

After charging for 2s, release pulses that damage enemies and heal you based on the damage done. While channeling Last Stand you have greatly increased bullet and spirit resist.Immune to fear while Last Stand is active.

Against it: It takes time to charge, so get out of the radius meanwhile

Gun
Warden's gun

Single shot · Ammo 17 · 3.8 bullets/sec · DPS 66 · full damage to 18m, minimum from 47m

Playing against

  • When Binding Word hits you, move far from that spot at once
  • When Last Stand starts charging, get out of the radius
  • Back off a bit when the flask cuts your gun damage

Counter items

Healbane

Strengths

  • Flask lowers enemy weapon damage and move speed
  • Binding Word locks enemies down and cuts off escapes
  • Last Stand holds out in the middle of team fights

Weaknesses

  • Binding Word can be escaped by moving at once
  • Last Stand can be avoided by leaving the radius while it charges
  • Willpower is his only movement, so he's weak to poke

Game plan

Laning

Cut enemy firepower with the flask and trade aggressively.

Mid game

Bind enemies with Binding Word and set up kills with allies.

Late game

Go in first and hold out with Last Stand to break up the enemy.

Power spikes

Alchemical Flask wins him early trades. Once he has Last Stand, he holds out in the middle of team fights and deals big damage.

With them on your team

  • Deal damage right away to enemies Warden binds
  • Fight by Warden's side during Last Stand
