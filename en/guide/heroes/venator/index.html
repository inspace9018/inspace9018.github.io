Abilities

1. Consecrating Grenade Radius 4.5m

Fire a grenade that bounces before exploding, dealing weapon damage and setting enemies on fire. Burning targets deal pure damage to enemies in the area and suffer from reduced healing.

Against it: When you burn, nearby allies take damage too, so spread out

2. Gutshot 10m

Fire a blast with your shotgun, dealing weapon damage and pushing enemies back. Enemies near a wall receive stun and take bonus weapon damage.

Against it: Don't stand with your back to a wall

3. Hex-Lined Snap Trap Radius 2m

Kick a trap that arms after a brief delay. The trap springs on the first enemy it touches, dealing spirit damage, applying immobilize, and revealing enemies for a duration afterwards.

Against it: A trap reveals you, so take cover as soon as it ends

4. Ira Domini Radius 0.2m

Load your crossbow with 3 stakes, dealing massively increased weapon damage.The final stake is blessed, dealing bonus pure damage and executing low-health enemies.

Against it: When he raises the crossbow, don't stand in front of him while low

Gun
Venator's gun

Single shot · Ammo 33 · 7.9 bullets/sec · DPS 63 · full damage to 18m, minimum from 47m

Playing against

  • Don't fight with your back to a wall; face him in the open
  • Kicked traps arm after a moment, so keep away from them
  • When low, get out of sight to avoid the crossbow execute

Counter items

Bullet ResilienceMetal Skin

Strengths

  • Grenade burn reduces enemy healing
  • Gutshot stuns enemies near walls
  • Ultimate's last stake can execute

Weaknesses

  • Traps and grenades lose value once their spots are read
  • Little mobility, weak to ambushes
  • Missing the ultimate loses much of its power

Game plan

Laning

Poke with the grenade and shotgun blast, and push enemies toward walls for stuns.

Mid game

Block chokepoints with traps and catch trapped enemies with your team.

Late game

After team fights, execute low enemies with Ira Domini stakes.

Power spikes

After Ira Domini, he can execute low enemies even from range. Each stake hits harder as gun items stack up.

With them on your team

  • Burning enemies heal less, so focus them then
  • Pour abilities onto enemies caught in a trap
