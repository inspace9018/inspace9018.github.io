Abilities

1. Powder Keg Radius 6m

Throw a barrel that explodes after a delay, dealing spirit damage, setting enemies on fire and applying knockup. The barrel can be detonated early by being shot, melee'd, or detonated by another barrel. The burn stacks, so overlapping barrels burn for their combined damage.Can be Alt-Cast to place the barrel in front of Holliday.

Against it: It explodes after a delay, so dodge as soon as you see the barrel

2. Bounce Pad Radius 9m

Drop a bounce pad in the world that launches any hero. You explode on landing, dealing Damage to any nearby enemies. The explosion can only occur once per person per Bounce Pad.

Against it: She flies in off the pad and explodes on landing, so keep away from pads

3. Crackshot Radius 2m

Headshots deal bonus Damage and applies a Fading Move Speed penalty. This effect can only occur when off cooldown. Cooldown is reduced by 50% on NPC hits. Crackshot ignores range damage fall-off and does not apply to objectives.

Against it: Headshots slow you, so don't move in a straight line

4. Spirit Lasso 20m

Throw out your lasso, dealing spirit damage, pulling, and applying stun.Using a Bounce Pad extends the duration of Spirit Lasso.

Against it: It pulls and stuns you: keep your distance from Holliday and stay near cover

Gun
Holliday's gun

Single shot · Ammo 10 · 2.1 bullets/sec · DPS 42 · full damage to 20m, minimum from 58m

Playing against

  • Keep moving instead of standing still to dodge headshots
  • When a Powder Keg lands, get away before it explodes
  • A placed Bounce Pad is an ambush route, so watch it

Counter items

Bullet Resilience

Strengths

  • Crackshot headshots poke at any range
  • Bounce Pad makes ambushes and escapes easy
  • Spirit Lasso pulls enemies in and stuns them

Weaknesses

  • Damage swings a lot with headshot skill
  • Powder Keg gives time to dodge before it blows
  • Struggles in close brawls

Game plan

Laning

Chip enemy health with headshots and clear waves with Powder Keg.

Mid game

Take high ground with Bounce Pad to poke, and look for ambushes in other lanes.

Late game

Deal damage with headshots from range and lasso in enemy damage dealers.

Power spikes

Crackshot makes her strong from the start as long as she lands headshots. Once Spirit Lasso comes in, Bounce Pad ambushes reliably pick off one enemy.

With them on your team

  • Ride Holliday's Bounce Pad with her to join the ambush
  • Focus the enemy pulled in by the lasso right away
