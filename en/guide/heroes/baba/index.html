Abilities

1. Threadsap 35m

Weave a scraggly thread that follows your crosshair before bursting along its path, dealing spirit damage and stealing enemy Spirit, increased in effectiveness for Baba.While holding stolen Spirit, Baba deals bonus spirit damage with every bullet.

Against it: Step off the path before the thread bursts

2. Granny Long Legs 2m

Your bench's legs extend, granting bonus move speed, higher jumps, Debuff Resistance and Melee Resistance.Light Melee: Perform a kick that knocks enemies back.Heavy Melee: Perform a ground pound that applies knockup, slow, and deals spirit damage.Hold Crouch / slide to charge a high jump, release to jump.

Against it: Don't get close to Baba while her legs are extended

3. Baba's Brew 12m

Brew a pot of tea that applies a magical effect in an area when thrown, cycling over time between a soothing barrier, Silence, or scalding burn that deals spirit damage over time and reduces spirit resist. Press to pause or resume brewing.

Against it: Leave the tea area to avoid the silence and burn

4. Feed The Birds 18m

Cover targets in front of you with bread, adding a stacking slow .When the channel ends a flock of pigeons bombard the targets, dealing spirit damage and hexing each target into a pigeon, increasing in both damage and duration based on bread count.

Against it: Get out from in front of Baba before the bread stacks up

Gun
Baba's gun

3 pellets · Ammo 15 · 3.2 bullets/sec · DPS 85 · full damage to 15m, minimum from 42m

Playing against

  • Step off the thread's path so she can't steal your Spirit
  • Bread stacking up is dangerous, so leave Feed The Birds' range fast
  • When her legs extend, keep your distance from kicks and ground pounds

Counter items

Debuff ReducerSpirit Resilience

Strengths

  • Threadsap steals enemy Spirit to make her stronger
  • Her tea both protects allies and silences
  • Ultimate turns several enemies into pigeons

Weaknesses

  • Hard to time the tea effects
  • Ultimate is a forward cone, so it's weak if dodged
  • Her damage drops when Threadsap misses

Game plan

Laning

Steal enemy Spirit with Threadsap to boost your bullet damage and poke.

Mid game

Pick the right tea to protect allies or silence enemies.

Late game

Turn several enemies into pigeons with Feed The Birds, then go in with your team.

Power spikes

Her bullet Spirit damage jumps while she holds Spirit stolen with Threadsap. Feed The Birds lets her turn grouped enemies into pigeons and open team fights.

With them on your team

  • Stay close so you can catch Baba's barrier tea
  • Focus enemies turned into pigeons right away
