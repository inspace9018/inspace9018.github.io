Abilities

1. Siphon Life Radius 8m

Drain health from nearby enemies, dealing spirit damage over time and healing for a portion of the damage dealt.

Against it: The closer you are, the more health he drains, so get away

2. Shoulder Charge

Charge forward, pulling enemies you hit. Pushing a hero into a wall applies stun.If you collide with a hero you move faster during your charge.

Against it: Don't put your back to a wall; step aside from the charge path

3. Infernal Resilience

Gain bonus defensive attributes. Taking damage grants temporary regeneration for a portion of the damage taken.

Against it: He heals as he takes hits, so burst him fast or use healing reduction

4. Seismic Impact Radius 9m

Leap high into the air before crashing into the ground, dealing spirit damage and applying stun.

Against it: When he leaps into the air, dash away from the landing spot

Gun
Abrams's gun

9 pellets · Ammo 9 · 1.6 bullets/sec · DPS 51 · full damage to 17m, minimum from 40m

Playing against

  • Don't stand with your back to a wall, to avoid the Shoulder Charge stun
  • Back away as soon as you're in Siphon Life range
  • After he charges, keep your distance and poke

Counter items

HealbaneToxic Bullets

Strengths

  • Lasts long with Siphon Life and regeneration
  • Shoulder Charge pins enemies into walls for a stun
  • Seismic Impact engages team fights from far away

Weaknesses

  • Weak to ranged poke that keeps distance
  • Healing reduction cuts his staying power a lot
  • Hard to chase enemies once his charge is used

Game plan

Laning

Push enemies with Siphon Life and melee, and set up kills with Shoulder Charge.

Mid game

Join the next lane with Seismic Impact and start fights.

Late game

Go in first, lock down enemy carries and buy time for your team's damage.

Power spikes

Shoulder Charge and Siphon Life make his early close-range fights strong. Seismic Impact completes his long-range engage and stun combo, and he gets tougher as Vitality items stack up.

With them on your team

  • Pour damage on enemies Abrams drags in with his charge
  • Attack safely from behind while Abrams takes hits up front
