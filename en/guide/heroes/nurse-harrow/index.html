Abilities

1. Leech Therapy Radius 6.5m

Throw a jar of leeches that attach to enemies, dealing spirit damage over time and eating away at the duration of enemy buffs.

Against it: Leeches make buffs end faster, so use yours later

2. Wretched Vapors 13m

Crack open a vial to release an ethereal vapor onto yourself or an ally that causes a rush, then a crash.Specter Rush: While it lasts, increases the speed of all actions and temporarily numbs all incoming damage creating looming specters.Specter Crash: When the vapors wear off, all actions are slower and any remaining specters deal their delayed damage plus extra damage.Incoming healing will prior

Against it: Targets slow down after the vapor ends, so attack then

3. Healing Needles

Load your weapon with a full clip of syringes that heal allies. Dealing damage recovers charges, and each leech you have collected slowly restores them.Reloading spends a charge for a fresh clip.Unused syringes are refunded as a partial charge. Recast to put them away.

Against it: Go for Harrow before the enemy she's healing

4. Lurking Terrors 35m

Open your bag and reveal the horrors you carry inside, dealing spirit damage over time to enemies in front of you. Enemies looking at you build up terror and cannot use stamina or movement abilities. At max terror, they flee in fear away from you.Each leech you have collected adds fear duration.Enemies facing away from you take 50% damage unless feared.

Against it: When the bag opens, don't look at Harrow; turn away and leave

Gun

Single shot · Ammo 11 · 2.6 bullets/sec · DPS 39 · full damage to 20m, minimum from 58m

Playing against

  • Turn your back when Harrow opens her bag to stop terror building up
  • Counterattack the target slowed after the vapor wears off
  • Save important buffs while leeches are on you

Counter items

HealbaneSpirit Resilience

Strengths

  • Syringe clip heals allies
  • Leeches shorten enemy buffs
  • Ultimate terrifies enemies who look at her

Weaknesses

  • Every action slows down when the vapor wears off
  • Her damage drops when she focuses on healing
  • Ultimate damage is weak against enemies facing away

Game plan

Laning

Chip enemies with leeches and refill ally health with syringes to hold the lane.

Mid game

Put the vapor on an ally right before a fight for a burst of damage.

Late game

Scatter enemies with Lurking Terrors and keep allies alive with syringes.

Power spikes

The more leeches she collects, the more syringe charges and terror duration she gets. Lurking Terrors lets her drive enemies off in team fights.

With them on your team

  • Fight within Harrow's sight so her syringes can reach you
  • When you get the vapor, burst while you're fast and leave before it ends
