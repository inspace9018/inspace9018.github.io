Abilities

1. Essence Bomb Radius 7m

Sacrifice some of your health to launch a bomb that deals damage after a brief arm time.Self damage type is Spirit and can be mitigated.

Against it: It takes time to go off after landing, so leave the spot right away

2. Life Drain 18m

Create a tether that drains enemy health over time and heals you. Target must be in line of sight and within max range to drain. You can shoot and use other abilities during the drain, but your move speed is reduced. to give health to friendly Heroes.

Against it: Break line of sight or leave its range to cut the drain

3. Malice

Sacrifice some of your health to launch blood shards that apply a stack of Malice. Each stack slows the victim and increases the damage they take from you. The slow effect decreases over time.

Against it: Damage taken grows with each stack, so back off after several hits

4. Soul Exchange 5.5m

Swaps health levels with an enemy target. There is a minimum health percentage that enemies can be brought down to and a minimum amount of health received based on victims current health.

Against it: It swaps health, so stay away from Geist when her health is low

Gun
Lady Geist's gun

Single shot · Ammo 9 · 2.1 bullets/sec · DPS 44 · full damage to 17m, minimum from 48m

Playing against

  • When Life Drain connects, go behind a wall or move far away
  • Essence Bomb has a delay before it goes off, so move at once
  • Don't walk up to a low-health Geist while you're at full health

Counter items

HealbaneSpirit Shielding

Strengths

  • Essence Bomb pokes the wave and heroes together
  • Life Drain deals damage and restores health
  • Soul Exchange turns losing fights around

Weaknesses

  • Abilities cost health, so poke wears her down
  • Life Drain breaks out of sight or out of range
  • Very weak to healing reduction

Game plan

Laning

Poke with Essence Bomb and refill lost health with Life Drain.

Mid game

Weaken enemies with Malice, then burst them down with your team.

Late game

Steal the enemy carry's health with Soul Exchange to turn team fights.

Power spikes

Essence Bomb and Life Drain give her good early poke and healing. Once she has Soul Exchange, she can turn fights even at low health.

With them on your team

  • Keep enemies from breaking Geist's Life Drain
  • Follow up right after Geist uses Soul Exchange to finish the target
