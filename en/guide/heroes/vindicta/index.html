Abilities

1. Stake 9m

Throw a stake that tethers enemies to the location where the stake lands. Enemy movement is restricted to the length of the tether.

Against it: Dodge sideways when you see the throw; if tethered, take cover inside its range

2. Flight

Leap into the air and fly. While in flight your weapon deals bonus spirit damage and your items have increased range.

Against it: Focus fire while she flies and she drops fast

3. Crow Familiar

Your crow familiar deals impact damage, reduces their bullet resist and applies a bleed that deals damage based on the target's current health.

Against it: Bleed scales with your current health, so avoid risky fights after a hit

4. Assassinate very far

Use your scoped rifle to fire a powerful shot over long distances. Deal only partial damage until fully charged after 1.0s of being scoped. Does bonus damage to enemies with less than 50% health remaining. Landing a killing blow on a player with Assassinate grants you bonus weapon damage.

Against it: Below half health, don't stand long where a sniper has an angle

Gun
Vindicta's gun

Single shot · Ammo 19 · 4.3 bullets/sec · DPS 53 · full damage to 20m, minimum from 64m

Playing against

  • When Vindicta is flying, focus your guns together to bring her down
  • Below half health, take cover instead of standing in the open
  • Close the gap and force a close fight right after she uses Stake

Counter items

Metal SkinBullet Resilience

Strengths

  • Attacks safely from high up while in Flight
  • Assassinate secures kills from long range
  • Stake stops enemies from escaping

Weaknesses

  • Fragile and easy to catch up close
  • Becomes a focus target while airborne
  • Lacks a way out once Stake is used

Game plan

Laning

Poke with the crow and your gun, and focus on last hits.

Mid game

Fly from high ground and add ranged damage to your team's fights.

Late game

Stay behind your team and pick off low-health enemies with Assassinate.

Power spikes

Flight and Crow Familiar raise her poke from the mid game, and Assassinate lets her pick off low-health enemies from far away. She is most dangerous late, once weapon items stack up.

With them on your team

  • Pour damage on enemies tethered by Vindicta's Stake
  • Vindicta is fragile, so peel for her when enemies get close
