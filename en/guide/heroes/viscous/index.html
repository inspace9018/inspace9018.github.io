Abilities

1. Splatter Radius 5m

Throw a ball of goo that deals damage and leaves puddles of goo behind that apply movement slow to enemies in the radius.

Against it: Staying in the puddle keeps you slowed, so get out at once

2. The Cube 20m

Encase the target in a cube of restorative goo that protects from damage, and increases health regen. Target is unable to take any new actions while cubed. Can be used on self. Press Jump / mantle to escape early.

Against it: The Cube takes no damage, so stop shooting and wait for it to end

3. Puddle Punch 40m

Materialize a fist in the world that punches everyone in the area, applying knockup. Punching an enemy deals melee damage and applies slow. Enemies can Parry this attack.You and your allies have increased Air Control.

Against it: You can Parry the fist as it rises

4. Goo Ball Radius 1.4m

Morph into a large goo ball that deals damage and stuns enemies on impact. The ball grants large amounts of Bullet and Spirit resist, bounces off walls and can double jump.

Against it: The ball bounces off walls, so avoid walls and spread out

Gun
Viscous's gun

Single shot · Ammo 20 · 4.8 bullets/sec · DPS 49 · full damage to 20m, minimum from 58m

Playing against

  • Don't stand in goo puddles
  • Puddle Punch can be blocked with Parry, so time it
  • Wait for enemies in The Cube to come out before attacking

Counter items

Spirit Shielding

Strengths

  • Splatter pokes and clears waves
  • The Cube saves an ally or himself from danger
  • Goo Ball goes in tanky and stuns

Weaknesses

  • Can't do anything inside The Cube
  • Puddle Punch can be blocked with Parry
  • Fragile when not in ball form

Game plan

Laning

Clear waves with Splatter while slowing and poking the enemy.

Mid game

Knock enemies up with Puddle Punch and set up kills with allies.

Late game

Roll in with Goo Ball to stun and protect your key ally with The Cube.

Power spikes

Splatter gives strong early poke and wave clear. Once he has Goo Ball, high resists let him roll into team fights and stun.

With them on your team

  • Stay close to Viscous so he can Cube you when you're in danger
  • Deal damage right away to enemies stunned by Goo Ball
