Abilities

1. Frost Grenade Radius 6.5m

Throw a grenade that explodes in a cloud of freezing ice that heals allies and applies spirit damage and move speed reduction to enemies.

Against it: Damage and slow continue inside the cloud, so leave at once

2. Ice Path Radius 1.2m

Kelvin creates a floating trail of ice and snow that gives movement bonuses to him and his allies. Kelvin gains 60% slow resistance for the duration. Enemies can also walk on the floating trail. Press Dash / Crouch / slide to travel up or down while in Ice Path.

Against it: Enemies can ride the Ice Path too; use it to chase or escape

3. Arctic Beam 25m

Shoot freezing cold energy out in front of you, damaging targets and progressively reducing their movement and fire rate the longer you sustain the beam on them. You have reduced move speed while using Arctic Beam. The beam may also claim Souls.

Against it: Slow and fire rate loss grow the longer you're hit, so break line of sight

4. Frozen Shelter 8m

Target yourself or a Hero to freeze the air and create an impenetrable dome around them. While in the dome, allies gain rapid regeneration and enemies are slowed. Objectives become Invulnerable and Frozen under Frozen Shelter

Against it: Enemies in the dome heal fast, so keep your distance until it ends

Gun
Kelvin's gun

Single shot · Ammo 14 · 3.8 bullets/sec · DPS 71 · full damage to 20m, minimum from 58m

Playing against

  • Arctic Beam slows you more the longer it hits, so break it with cover
  • Get out of the Frost Grenade cloud at once
  • When Frozen Shelter goes up, don't force it; wait until it ends

Counter items

Healbane

Strengths

  • Frost Grenade deals damage and heals allies at once
  • Ice Path moves him and his allies fast
  • Frozen Shelter protects allies and objectives

Weaknesses

  • Slow while using Arctic Beam, an easy target
  • Lacks burst damage
  • Enemies can use Ice Path too

Game plan

Laning

Hold the lane, using Frost Grenade for poke and healing.

Mid game

Bring allies along Ice Path and join other lanes fast.

Late game

Hold team fights by shielding falling allies or objectives with Frozen Shelter.

Power spikes

Frost Grenade gives him poke and healing from the start. Once he has Frozen Shelter, his team fights and objective defense get much stronger.

With them on your team

  • Ride Kelvin's Ice Path to engage or retreat together
  • Step inside Frozen Shelter to heal when in danger
