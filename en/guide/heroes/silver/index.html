Abilities

1. Slam Fire 1m

Instantly reload your gun. You gain bonus fire rate and deal bonus weapon damage based on the target's health for a limited number of shots, but suffer from reduced accuracy.Deals bonus Weapon Damage against troopers and neutrals.

Against it: Her accuracy is low during Slam Fire, so get distance to take less damage

2. Boot Kick 10.6m

Against it: Don't stay at melee range for long; keep your distance

3. Entangling Bola

Throw a bola, dealing spirit damage, applying slow and preventing movement abilities or stamina.

Against it: Strafe left and right to dodge the bola

4. Lycan Curse

Passive: Dealing damage generates bloodlust, increased at low health. At max bloodlust, you automatically cast Lycan Curse.Active: Instantly transform, gaining increased max health, and stacking fire rate on enemy heroes, and replacing your abilities and weapon with their ferocious versions.

Against it: Avoid head-on fights while she's transformed and stall until it ends

Gun
Silver's gun

7 pellets · Ammo 7 · 1.2 bullets/sec · DPS 42 · full damage to 17m, minimum from 42m

Playing against

  • A bola hit stops your movement abilities, so keep your distance beforehand
  • Back off as soon as Silver transforms and wait out the duration
  • Bloodlust fills fast when Silver is low, so finish her quickly

Counter items

Slowing HexBullet Resilience

Strengths

  • Slam Fire clears lanes and jungle fast
  • Bola stops movement abilities and Stamina
  • Transforming gives a big boost to health and fire rate

Weaknesses

  • Accuracy drops during Slam Fire
  • Weak against enemies poking from range
  • Only average durability before transforming

Game plan

Laning

Clear Troopers fast with Slam Fire and pressure enemies held by the bola.

Mid game

Farm neutrals quickly to grow and pick fights when you can transform.

Late game

Turn into a wolf, jump into the enemy team and shred them.

Power spikes

When bloodlust fills and Lycan Curse triggers, her health and fire rate jump. Slam Fire clears Troopers and neutrals fast, so she grows quickly.

With them on your team

  • Focus enemies held by the bola together
  • Go in with Silver when she transforms and start the fight
