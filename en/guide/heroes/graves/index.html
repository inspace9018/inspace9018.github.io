Abilities

1. Jar of Dead Radius 7m

Passive: Collect death when anything dies nearby and store it in your Jar of Dead.Active: Throw a jar to summon Deadheads that repeatedly deal spirit damage to enemies. Deadheads follow you instead if there's no nearby enemies, and prioritize the target of your weapon.

Against it: Get out of the spot when Deadheads are summoned

2. Grasping Hands 24m

Unearth a line of grasping hands, summoning a Ghoul and leaving behind a rift. Enemies who pass through take spirit damage and receive immobilize. Alt-Cast rotates the orientation of the wall.

Against it: Don't walk over the rift where the hands rise

3. Essence Theft

Your weapon steals weapon damage and spirit resist over time, up to a maximum amount per target.

Against it: Don't take his fire for long; break line of sight to stop the theft

4. Borrowed Decree 20m

Against it: Keep your health high and stay at range when his ultimate is ready

Gun
Graves's gun

Single shot · Ammo 40 · 9.8 bullets/sec · DPS 35 · full damage to 8m, minimum from 17m

Playing against

  • Go around the Grasping Hands rift or wait until it's gone
  • Deadheads go for whoever Graves shoots, so back off when you're the target
  • The longer you get shot, the more resist he steals, so keep fights short

Counter items

Spirit ResilienceDebuff Reducer

Strengths

  • Deadheads deal steady Spirit damage
  • Grasping Hands block paths and lock enemies down
  • His gun steals enemy damage and Spirit Resist

Weaknesses

  • Hard to use the jar with no death nearby
  • Grasping Hands are easy to see and avoid
  • Little mobility, weak to ambushes

Game plan

Laning

Kill Troopers to gather death and pressure enemies and Troopers with the jar.

Mid game

Block paths with Grasping Hands to help your team ambush and chase.

Late game

Shred enemy resists with Deadheads and Essence Theft and deal steady damage from the back.

Power spikes

Jar of Dead gets more dangerous the more death he gathers nearby. Long fights favor him as Essence Theft stacks up.

With them on your team

  • Clear Troopers with him to fill the jar with death
  • Focus fire on enemies held by Grasping Hands
