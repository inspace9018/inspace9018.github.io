Abilities

1. Pillow Toss 40m

Throw your trusty pillow inflicting spirit damage and heavy knockback.Landing hits reduces the cooldown of your other abilities.

Against it: The pillow knocks you far, so don't stand near dangerous drops

2. Tag Along 23m

Jump to an ally and nap alongside them. Both you and the ally receive a burst heal based on missing health followed by lingering regeneration.Hop between allies with Ability 2 to apply the heal, once per ally.You are knocked off if stunned by an ultimate ability.You can use items and all self-casts target your ally instead.

Against it: Target the hero Rem is on first, or stun him off

3. Lil Helpers 45m

Signal a Helper to lend a hand. They can collect boxes, do Sinner's Sacrifices, or be sent to support allies and troopers.Follow a hero: Provides a short burst of spirit resist and fading move speed.Follow a trooper: Provides healing, damage, resists, and allows tagging along. When no hero is around, souls from trooper kills are split amongst the team.Can be cast during Tag Along. Helpers will tak

Against it: Troopers with a Helper get stronger, so kill them first

4. Naptime 24m

Cast your gaze forward, piercing walls and floors. Enemies in the gaze are slowed, have reduced dash distance, and cannot use movement abilities.When the channel ends they fall asleep before waking up to a splitting headache that deals heavy spirit damage.

Against it: The gaze goes through walls, so don't feel safe behind one

Gun
Rem's gun

Single shot · Ammo 13 · 3.8 bullets/sec · DPS 62 · full damage to 20m, minimum from 58m

Playing against

  • Burst down the ally Rem is on faster than he can heal
  • Stay away from ledges so the pillow can't knock you off
  • If his gaze catches you, get out of its line before the channel ends

Counter items

HealbaneDebuff Reducer

Strengths

  • Tag Along heals him and his ally together
  • Pillow knocks enemies far back
  • Ultimate pierces walls and blocks movement abilities

Weaknesses

  • Weak in fights on his own
  • Gets knocked off his ally by a stun
  • In danger along with the ally he's on when they go down

Game plan

Laning

Poke with the pillow and hold the lane, healing with your ally through Tag Along.

Mid game

Collect boxes and Souls with Lil Helpers, and jump onto an ally when a fight starts.

Late game

Lock down several enemies with Naptime and stay on an ally, healing to the end.

Power spikes

Naptime lets him lock down enemies even through walls and deal big damage. Team Souls and support improve as he gets better with his Lil Helpers.

With them on your team

  • Play a bit more aggressively when Rem is on you; he heals you
  • Follow enemies knocked away by the pillow and finish them
