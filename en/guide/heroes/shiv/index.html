Abilities

1. Serrated Knives

Throw a knife that bleeds an enemy. Each additional hit adds a stack and refreshes the bleed duration, causing the bleed to increase per stack.

Against it: The bleed grows with each hit, so don't take hits in a row

2. Slice and Dice 12m

Perform a dash forward, damaging enemies along the path. Hit Enemies have their spirit resist reduced. This debuff can stack.

Against it: Getting hit lowers your Spirit Resist, so watch for follow-up abilities

3. Bloodletting

Take only a portion of incoming damage immediately and defer the rest to be taken over time. Activate to clear a portion of the deferred damage.

Against it: He takes the damage later, so drag the fight out and keep pressure

4. Killing Blow 12m

Against it: Shiv leaps in when you're low, so make distance early

Gun
Shiv's gun

6 pellets · Ammo 10 · 1.8 bullets/sec · DPS 52 · full damage to 20m, minimum from 41m

Playing against

  • Back off before the knife bleed stacks up
  • Stay away from Shiv when your health is low
  • Bloodletting holds back damage, so keep pressuring instead of short trades

Counter items

Healbane

Strengths

  • Serrated Knives bleed gives steady poke
  • Bloodletting spreads out damage taken so he lasts longer
  • Killing Blow finishes low-health enemies

Weaknesses

  • Has to get close, weak against long-range poke
  • Loses sustain when hit by healing reduction
  • Hard to escape once Slice and Dice is used

Game plan

Laning

Stack bleed with Serrated Knives and dash in and out with Slice and Dice.

Mid game

Join other lanes and finish kills with Killing Blow.

Late game

Hold on with Bloodletting, dive into the enemy and pick off low-health enemies.

Power spikes

Serrated Knives and Slice and Dice let him fight early, and Killing Blow lets him reliably finish low-health enemies. Bloodletting also helps him survive after going in.

With them on your team

  • Use Spirit abilities on enemies whose Spirit Resist Shiv cut with his dash
  • When an enemy is low, let Shiv finish them with Killing Blow
