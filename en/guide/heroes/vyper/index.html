Abilities

1. Screwjab Dagger

Throw a dagger, dealing spirit damage and applying slow. Every subsequent dagger against the same target stacks in spirit damage and slow.

Against it: After one dagger hits, take cover at once to break the stacks

2. Lethal Venom 10m

Inject a target with lethal venom. After a delay the venom triggers, dealing Spirit Damage. The damage is increased by the target's missing health.Lethal Venom will ignore Petrify's damage block.

Against it: Heal or get distance before the venom goes off

3. Slither

You have increased Slide Distance, can Slide up hills, and can turn faster while Sliding.

Against it: She slides up hills too, so don't relax on high ground

4. Petrifying Bola Radius 12m

Throw an explosive bola. On exploding, the bola Slows and Damages all enemies in the area. Direct hits deal Additional Damage and Petrify instead. Petrified units block all damage, but cannot take actions.

Against it: Stay out of the bola blast and get ready to counter as Petrify ends

Gun
Vyper's gun

Single shot · Ammo 24 · 14.3 bullets/sec · DPS 94 · full damage to 15m, minimum from 33m

Playing against

  • Move along cover so daggers can't hit you in a row
  • When envenomed, top up health first to soften the burst
  • She slides in fast, so always watch your flanks

Counter items

Spirit ResilienceDebuff Reducer

Strengths

  • Repeated dagger hits stack a heavy slow
  • Venom scales with missing health, great for finishing
  • Boosted Slide makes chasing and escaping fast

Weaknesses

  • Pressure drops a lot when daggers miss
  • Weak at holding up in close brawls
  • A badly used Petrify protects the enemy from damage

Game plan

Laning

Slow enemies with daggers, poke with your gun, and look for Lethal Venom finishes.

Mid game

Slide around the flank and finish low enemies with venom.

Late game

Hold dangerous enemies briefly with the bola and clear backline damage dealers first.

Power spikes

Once Lethal Venom is upgraded and gun items are in, she reliably finishes wounded enemies. Petrifying Bola lets her lock down a key enemy in team fights.

With them on your team

  • Petrified enemies take no damage, so attack right after it ends
  • Chase enemies with stacked dagger slows together
