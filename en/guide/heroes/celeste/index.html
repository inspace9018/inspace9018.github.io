Abilities

1. Light Eater 10m

Blast enemies in a cone in front of you with a flare of light. Blasted enemies take spirit damage when attacked by Celeste and provides her spirit lifesteal.

Against it: Don't stand in front of Celeste; avoid the flare's range

2. Dazzling Trick Radius 14m

Surround yourself in a protective prism. If the barrier is destroyed, it silences nearby enemies and deals a portion of the barrier as damage.

Against it: Break the prism by shooting it from a distance

3. Radiant Daggers 30m

Call down a beam of light from the sky. After a short duration, the beam will fully form, causing an explosion that deals spirit damage to all targets in the area. Celeste will receive a stacking buff that increases her spirit damage anytime this hits an enemy hero.

Against it: Get out of the area before the beam fully forms

4. Shining Wonder Radius 15.5m

Launch a deadly orb of light that deals spirit damage and applies slow and reduces Dash Distance on impact. The orb then bounces to the enemies within range. If no target is found, the orb will linger for a short duration while continuing to look for targets.Prioritizes enemy heroes when picking targets.

Against it: The orb bounces to heroes first, so don't stand too close to allies

Gun
Celeste's gun

Single shot · Ammo 8 · 1.7 bullets/sec · DPS 31 · full damage to 22m, minimum from 60m

Playing against

  • When a beam comes down from the sky, leave the area at once
  • Breaking the prism silences you, so don't break it up close
  • Getting hit by the flare lets Celeste lifesteal, so back off right away

Counter items

Spirit ResilienceHealbane

Strengths

  • Spirit damage keeps stacking as her beams land
  • Light Eater gives her Spirit Lifesteal
  • Her prism silences nearby enemies when it breaks

Weaknesses

  • Beams have a delay and are easy to dodge
  • Little mobility, weak to ambushes
  • Low damage before her stacks build up

Game plan

Laning

Land beams steadily to stack Spirit damage and sustain with the flare.

Mid game

Hit grouped enemies with beams and orbs to raise your team fight damage.

Late game

Rain beams and orbs from the back with your stacked Spirit damage.

Power spikes

Her Spirit damage keeps rising the more Radiant Daggers she lands on heroes. Shining Wonder lets her slow and whittle down grouped enemies all at once.

With them on your team

  • Herd enemies to where the beam will land
  • Chase enemies slowed by the orb together
