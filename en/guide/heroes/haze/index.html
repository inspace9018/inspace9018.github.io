Abilities

1. Sleep Dagger

Throw a dagger that damages and sleeps the target. Sleeping targets wake up shortly after being damaged. Throwing a Dagger does not break your invisibility. Sleep Dagger does not interrupt enemies' channeling abilities.

Against it: Damage wakes the target quickly, so shoot a sleeping ally to wake them

2. Smoke Bomb Radius 18m

Fade out of sight, becoming invisible and gaining sprint speed. Attacking removes invisibility, but using items does not. Close enemies can see through your invisibility.

Against it: Invisibility shows up close, so watch suspicious spots

3. Fixation

Shooting a target increases your bullet damage on that target. Gain one stack per bullet hit, three if the hit is a headshot.

Against it: Damage grows the longer you're hit, so break her fire with cover

4. Bullet Dance Radius 16m

Enter a flurry, firing your weapon at nearby enemies with perfect accuracy and added Bullet Damage.

Against it: Interrupt it with a stun or silence, and build bullet defense to survive

Gun
Haze's gun

Single shot · Ammo 25 · 9.5 bullets/sec · DPS 50 · full damage to 20m, minimum from 46m

Playing against

  • Poke her hard early to slow her growth
  • When Bullet Dance starts, interrupt it at once with a stun or silence
  • Invisibility shows up close, so keep an eye on your surroundings

Counter items

Metal SkinReturn Fire

Strengths

  • Fixation deals more damage the longer she shoots
  • Smoke Bomb makes approaching and escaping easy
  • Bullet Dance clears nearby enemies all at once

Weaknesses

  • Weak in early laning and before items
  • Bullet Dance is easily interrupted by stuns
  • Her invisibility shows up close

Game plan

Laning

Take last hits safely and avoid risky fights.

Mid game

Rotate through the jungle and waves to get weapon items fast.

Late game

Get behind enemies with Smoke Bomb and clean up fights with Bullet Dance.

Power spikes

Her laning is on the weak side. From the mid to late game, as weapon items stack up, Fixation and Bullet Dance make her firepower explode.

With them on your team

  • Burst down enemies Haze puts to sleep together in one go
  • Hold the lane for her early to buy Haze time to grow
