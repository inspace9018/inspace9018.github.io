Abilities

1. Mini Turret 20m

Deploy a turret that shoots enemies, dealing spirit damage over time. The turret expires after a limited lifetime.Turrets deal reduced damage to troopers and objectives.

Against it: Turrets don't last long, so wait outside their range or destroy them

2. Medicinal Specter 15m

Deploy a spirit that provides healing to nearby allies.

Against it: Pull enemies out of the healing range to fight them

3. Spectral Wall 50m

Cast a wall that applies slow on enemies it passes through. Erupt the wall to divide the terrain in half and damage nearby enemies. After casting, press or Ability 3 to erupt the wall early. Can be Destroyed with Melee Attacks

Against it: The wall breaks to melee, so smash it at once if you're trapped

4. Heavy Barrage 36m

Unleashes a volley of rockets that home in on a targeted location. McGinnis is slowed and is still able to use stamina.

Against it: Keep moving to leave the barrage area, and target the slowed McGinnis

Gun
McGinnis's gun

Single shot · Ammo 66 · 4.8 bullets/sec · DPS 29 · full damage to 20m, minimum from 58m

Playing against

  • When turrets go down, wait outside their range or destroy them first
  • Spectral Wall breaks to melee, so hit it at once if you're trapped
  • McGinnis is slowed during Heavy Barrage, so go in then

Counter items

Spirit Shielding

Strengths

  • Turrets keep up lane pressure even when she's away
  • Medicinal Specter keeps her and her allies alive
  • Spectral Wall splits enemies apart and slows them

Weaknesses

  • Turrets are short-lived and can be destroyed
  • No mobility, so she's vulnerable when dived
  • Slowed during Heavy Barrage, which makes her a target

Game plan

Laning

Drop turrets to push the wave and poke the enemy along with your gun.

Mid game

Control the area around objectives with turrets and walls, moving with your team.

Late game

Cut the enemy formation with the wall and pressure trapped enemies with Heavy Barrage.

Power spikes

Mini Turret makes early wave control and poke easy. Heavy Barrage lets her pressure wide areas in team fights, and duration and cooldown items keep turrets up longer.

With them on your team

  • Fight near McGinnis's turrets to win the damage race
  • Keep fighting inside Medicinal Specter's range
