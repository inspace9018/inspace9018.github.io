Abilities

1. Bashdown 4m

Slam your bat into the ground, pulling enemies down and dealing melee damage.The slam creates a shockwave that deals spirit damage and applies knockup.

Against it: Stay out of Bashdown range and watch for the follow-up after you're lifted

2. Rising Ram Radius 1.5m

Charge head-first into an enemy and send them into the air along with Billy.Cooldown reduced by 50% on impact.

Against it: Step aside when Rising Ram comes

3. Blasted

Passive: Melee hits restore ammo and inflict wrecked on the victim for 7.0s.

Against it: Keep your distance so his melee can't hit you

4. Chain Gang 12m

Chain nearby enemies to you. Chained enemies cannot use movement abilities and receive a heavy slow when they pull on the chain.After a delay, yank everyone towards Billy and deal spirit damage.The chain breaks if you lose line of sight for a short while.

Against it: Chains break after losing sight for a while, so go behind a wall

Gun
Billy's gun

Single shot · Ammo 30 · 11.8 bullets/sec · DPS 74 · full damage to 14m, minimum from 32m

Playing against

  • Poke from outside Rising Ram range
  • Melee hits leave you wrecked, so don't get close
  • When chained, go around cover to break line of sight

Counter items

Slowing HexUnstoppable

Strengths

  • Rising Ram closes in fast and lifts enemies
  • Melee restores ammo, strong even in long fights
  • Chains stop enemy movement abilities

Weaknesses

  • Must get close, weak to ranged poke
  • Hard to engage if the charge misses
  • Very weak to slows and crowd control

Game plan

Laning

Close in with Rising Ram and melee for short, hard trades.

Mid game

Lift lone enemies with Rising Ram and catch them with your team.

Late game

Bind several enemies with Chain Gang and drag them toward allied fire.

Power spikes

After Chain Gang, he can bind and drag several enemies at once for strong team fight openers. With melee items, his melee pressure gets very strong.

With them on your team

  • When Billy lifts enemies, shoot them in the air right away
  • Land AoE abilities on enemies dragged in by the chains
